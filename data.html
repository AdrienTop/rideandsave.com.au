Fuel consumption data

Official figures for
Australia's most popular vehicles

Official fuel consumption from standardised WLTP lab tests, with real-world estimates based on mandatory on-board monitoring of 600,000 vehicles — published by the European Commission. Vehicle rankings by registered fleet size (BITRE 2024).

Where this data comes from

Vehicle rankings and registration counts come from the BITRE Road Vehicles Australia 2024 dataset — Australia's most comprehensive registered fleet snapshot (21.7 million vehicles). Fuel consumption figures are standardised WLTP lab test results, as published by the Green Vehicle Guide.

How we estimate real-world consumption

Official figures come from standardised lab conditions — no hills, no AC, no traffic. The European Commission published real-world data from mandatory on-board fuel consumption monitors (OBFCM) fitted to 600,000 vehicles. The gap between lab and road is +23.7% for petrol, +18.1% for diesel, and +14% for hybrids.   → EU Commission OBFCM Report (March 2024)
